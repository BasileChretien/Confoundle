Confoundle

What we store, and why

Confoundle works without an account. If you never sign in, this app sends nothing about you anywhere, and there is no record of you to speak of.

The short version

  • No account: your streak, your answers and your review schedule live in your own browser's storage. They never leave the device. There is nothing for us to look at, hand over or lose.
  • With an account: we store your email address and your spaced-repetition schedule, so your reviews follow you between devices. That is the entire list.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no profiling, no third-party analytics, and nothing is ever sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete everything yourself, from inside the app, immediately, with no request and no waiting period.

What is deliberately not collected

No name, no profile picture, no contacts, no location, no device fingerprint, no advertising identifier, no behavioural profile. The app's analytics module is a stub that does nothing. Your puzzle answers, your streak and your score stay on your own device even when you are signed in: they are not part of what an account syncs.

Signing in with Google

Google's sign-in script is loaded only when you open the account panel on a deployment that offers it. If you never do, this app never contacts Google at all. There is no automatic prompt.

If you do use it, Google hands the app a signed token identifying you. We check that token, take the account identifier and the email address from it, and discard the rest. We ask for no permissions on your Google account, receive no access token, and can read nothing else from it. In that exchange Google acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

Where the data sits, and who else touches it

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database, as a processor acting on our instructions. Like any web host it briefly sees the usual request metadata, including network addresses, under its own policy.
  • The email provider configured for this deployment delivers the sign-in code, as a processor. It sees the address the code is sent to.
  • Google, only if you choose the Google button, as described above.

These services operate globally, so data may be processed outside the European Economic Area under the transfer safeguards in their own terms. Nobody else receives any of it, and none of it is sold, rented or used for advertising.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to your data, correction of it, erasure, portability, restriction, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Two of those are wired into the app so you do not have to ask anyone:

  • Take a copy. Open the account panel and choose Download my data. The file contains everything the server holds about you, in a format the app itself can read back.
  • Erase everything. Open the account panel and choose Delete my account. This removes your account, your reviews and your sessions immediately, on the server and on the device you are using. There is no soft delete, no grace period and no way for us to restore it afterwards.

For anything else, write to the address above. If you are in the EU or EEA you may also complain to your national data protection authority; in France that is the CNIL. Users in Japan have the equivalent rights of disclosure, correction and deletion under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information, and may contact the Personal Information Protection Commission.

Children

Confoundle is not aimed at children and asks for no age information. Please do not create an account if you are under 16, or under the age your country sets for consenting to online services. The app itself is fully playable without one.

Security

There is no password to steal: we store no password hashes, because signing in means proving you control an email address rather than remembering a secret. Sign-in tokens and email codes are stored only as one-way digests, so a copy of the database would not let anyone sign in as you. Traffic is encrypted in transit.

Changes

The app is open source, so every change to what it stores is visible in its history. Material changes to this page will be noted here with a date.
